# Talking Skin

Gives every player in **Simple Voice Chat** thick cartoon lips with teeth that open with the volume of their voice,
plus subtle body, head and arm movement while they talk.

- Big, outlined lips and teeth that stick out of the face (also visible with helmets)
- Mouth opens by loudness, smooth open/close
- Light body bounce, head nods and hand gestures (only when the hand is free)
- Works for your own character (third person) and for everyone you hear
- Client-side only: other players do not need the mod
- Config: `config/talkingskin.json` (colors, lip thickness, gesture strength, toggles)

**Requires:** Fabric API, Simple Voice Chat (recommended)
